How to run it

  1. Download and unzip Nexora on your Windows laptop.
  2. Install Python once from python.org (free) — tick "Add python.exe to PATH" while installing.
  3. Double-click run_nexora.bat — the needed libraries install themselves the first time.
  4. Press the mic button, allow microphone permission, and speak. No mic? Type the command and press Do It.

Honest note: this is a test version. It needs internet for voice recognition, MS Word must be installed for Word to open, and it follows the commands listed above — it does not yet have an AI brain for open conversation.

Version 4 is also stricter: Nexora only acts on a clear command. An unclear sentence does nothing — never the wrong thing.

Online or Offline?

Nexora is an ONLINE voice assistant. Its voice works through the internet, so your internet must be ON when you speak — that is how Nexora hears and understands you.

Some typed commands for programs already on your laptop — like opening Notepad or Calculator — can also work offline. But voice, YouTube and search always need internet.

Simple rule: keep the internet ON, and everything works.
