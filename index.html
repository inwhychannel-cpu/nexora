New in v4 — Floating Nexora Icon

A small Nexora icon stays on top of every window on your laptop. Drag it anywhere, tap it, and give a 🎤 voice or ⌨ text command — wherever you are.

Online or offline?

Nexora is an ONLINE voice assistant — voice needs the internet ON. Some typed commands for programs on your laptop also work offline. Simple rule: keep the internet ON, and everything works.
