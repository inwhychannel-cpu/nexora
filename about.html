About Nexora

Nexora is a small, free Windows program that runs your laptop by voice. You speak a simple command — Nexora opens programs, searches the web, types for you, and speaks back to confirm.

Everything Nexora does

  • Opens programs: Word, Chrome, Notepad, Calculator, Paint, File Explorer
  • Opens your folders: Pictures, Videos, Downloads and more
  • Searches Google and YouTube by voice
  • Types what you say (dictation) into any program
  • Tells you the time and the date
  • Controls volume (up, down, mute) and closes the current window
  • Floating icon: a small Nexora icon stays on top of every window — drag it anywhere
  • Speaks back and shows every action on screen

Languages

Nexora listens in 62 languages — pick yours from the list. In 21 languages it also gives full spoken replies; in the others it replies in English.

Online first

Nexora hears your voice through the internet, so keep your internet ON when you speak. YouTube and search need internet too. A few typed commands for programs already on your laptop also work offline.

Honest note — test version

Nexora is a free test version (Rs 0). It has been carefully tested, but the first real test is on your own Windows laptop. Windows may show a one-time “unknown publisher” message for new apps — choose “More info” → “Run anyway” to start Nexora.
